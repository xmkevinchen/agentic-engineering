/ae:go · stage contracts

Two Gates, Five Stages

Each stage has one responsibility, consumes the stage before it, and produces one deliverable of its own. A stage may refuse what it is given and send it back. The human sets the goal and signs twice; everything else belongs to the loop.

01

Analyze /ae:analyze 38 lines

Establish that the problem is real and settle what done means, before any solution exists.

delivers
<feature-dir>/analysis.md
must hold
The premise is answered and every answer carries evidence — a path, a line, or a command someone else can run. The criteria are settled here, each with an id, the property, and the falsifier.
refused for
  • a criterion with no falsifier and no judgement mark
  • a premise answer with no evidence behind it
exit
A no on the first premise question ends the item. That is a result, not a failure.
02

Discuss /ae:discuss 52 lines optional

Settle one decision where two defensible options lead to materially different work. Nothing contested → skip it; a discussion held as ceremony costs a cycle and decides nothing.

delivers
one decision record in <feature-dir>/
must hold
The losing option is argued, not merely listed. The reason is evidence a reader can open. It names what would reopen it — an observation someone could actually make.
refused for
  • a question it opened is still open
  • the reason cites nothing a reader can open
  • the record exists only in the conversation
03

Plan /ae:plan 45 lines

Decide the method. The criteria themselves are not yours to change — they arrive word for word from the analysis.

delivers
<feature-dir>/plan.md
must hold
A dependency-ordered stack where each step is one commit that closes on itself. Coverage runs both ways: every criterion served, no step building what no criterion asks for. No criterion rests on a check nobody has seen fail.
gate
Present the criteria first — that is the thing being confirmed. The step cut is advisory context. Work does not start without it.
refused for
  • a step that names no check to turn red
  • a criterion whose text differs from the analysis
04

Work /ae:work 58 lines

Turn an approved plan into commits, leaving behind the evidence that each criterion's check can actually fail.

delivers
commits, plus <feature-dir>/log.md
must hold
One step, one commit. Every check was seen failing before the work that made it pass — watch it go red, or plant a defect once it is green. A criterion nothing can check is recorded as unchecked, never reported satisfied.
gate
None. Stop only when a criterion must change; everything else is yours to decide, note, and continue.
refused for
  • a criterion's check was never seen red
  • files changed that no step accounts for
05

Review /ae:review 48 lines

Judge the delivered work against the criteria the human confirmed. Nothing else.

delivers
<feature-dir>/review.md
fresh eyes
A reader who did not write the work. How many and who is your call; that they did not write it is not. Nobody signs off their own work.
must hold
Every verdict rests on evidence this review produced — the executor's green run is a claim, not evidence. The checks bite: break what the most load-bearing criterion protects and confirm it catches you. What was not checked is listed plainly.
gate
Done means the human signed — not tests green, not your own pass verdict. A gate the executed party can open is not a gate.
returns
Implementation defects go back to WORK — the ordinary loop. A finding that would change what a criterion means goes back to ANALYZE via the human.

Where the instruments went. None of these stages carries a mechanical check. The flow was compressed first and deliberately over-subtracted; instruments come back one at a time, each admitted only when the harm from its absence has been observed rather than argued. The preserved originals and the add-back list live beside the run record.

The entry runs it. /ae:go invokes each stage’s skill in turn, then reads that stage’s deliverable off disk and checks it against what the next stage would refuse it for — a stage that would be refused is sent back at once, not discovered three stages later. What a stage does is in that stage’s own skill and is never re-derived at the entry: 108 lines of programme over 241 lines of stage contract, from 3,438 before the cut.
